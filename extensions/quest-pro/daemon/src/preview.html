    Extra recordings optional

    Use these if something still tracks badly after training. They add to your basic recording rather than replace it.

    Follow the dotFollow a moving dot with the tip of your tongue, with short tongue-in rests between. It covers every direction, including the ones in between, so it's the best way to keep adding data. About 2½ min.
    Direction posesHalfway and diagonal directions. Use this if left–right or up–down jumps straight to the extremes. About 2 min.
    Expression posesSmiles, vowels and puffed cheeks, mostly with your tongue in. Use this if the tongue shows when it shouldn't. About 2 min.

    You can keep using VRFaceTracking meanwhile. Your current model stays on until the new one is ready.

    Training options

    Automatic uses the GPU (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel) when one works, and the CPU if not. The CPU is much slower. More passes take longer.
